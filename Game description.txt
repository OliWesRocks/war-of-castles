Castles at War
Conquer the medieval world one region at a time in this turn-based territory strategy game.
Every game begins on a freshly generated map — no two wars are ever the same. Expand your kingdom by seizing villages for taxes and castles for recruiting armies, then crush your rivals before time runs out.
Choose your faction and play to your strengths: Merchants grow rich fast, Warriors hit harder in battle, Stoics are stronger at defence, and Ninjas get extra move. Outsmart three AI opponents across a unique procedurally generated landscape every time you play.
While you pursue dominance over the map, the world has its own plans. Floods cut off coastal regions, wildfires sweep through inland regions devastating stationed armies, and a Rich Harvest makes all attacking armies stronger.

Features:
Procedurally generated maps — a new battlefield every game
4 asymmetric factions with unique passives
Deterministic combat with a twist of fate on tied battles
World event system that affects the map each round
Four session lengths: Quick Skirmish, Standard, and Marathon
Two victory paths: Conquest or Economic dominance
Auto-save — pick up exactly where you left off
