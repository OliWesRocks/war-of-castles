How to Play

Goal.
Conquer the realm. Win by holding the most regions when time runs out, or be the first to amass 50 coins.

Each turn you get 3 moves.
One move = march one army into a neighbouring region.

Castles produce 1 army unit per turn.
Villages produce 2 coins per turn.
Every 2 villages you own boosts one castle to produce an extra unit per turn.

Spend coins to recruit extra units or build palisades.
Palisades cost 3 coins and add +1 defence to any empty region. Maximum 3 per player.

Combat is decided by numbers — bigger army wins. Ties are decided by fate. Both sides take losses.

Your faction has a unique power:

Merchants: +1 coin per turn
Warriors: +1 attacking unit in battle
Stoics: +1 defending unit in battle
Ninjas: extra move every 3rd turn

Each round a World Event strikes — floods block coastal regions, wildfires burn the land, rich harvests power up your attacks. No event repeats twice in a row.

You are eliminated when you lose all armies and all castles.
